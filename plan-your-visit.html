Getting here

Champagne Island sits in the Placencia lagoon, just a short boat ride from the Placencia Peninsula. The trip takes roughly 5–10 minutes and offers a scenic view of the mangrove ecosystem — keep an eye out for manatees and birds along the way.

Boats depart from the Placencia Municipal Pier & Plaza (GJ7P+93G, Placencia Sidewalk). Pickup times and any costs are to confirm with the owners.

What to bring

Pack sun protection (sunscreen, hat, sunglasses), bug spray, and water shoes for the lagoon and trails. We recommend bringing cash for any purchases, as well as a reusable water bottle and a dry bag to keep valuables safe near the water.

If you plan to camp, bring a tent, sleeping pad, and bedding — these are to confirm as provided or not.

What is provided

Champagne Island offers guided tours, nature trails, and a butterfly house. The island has a Garifuna and Maya cultural exhibit and serves authentic Garifuna food prepared by Ms. Jackie. Water, sunscreen, and bug spray have been offered to guests during tours.

Details on bedding, towels, meals, drinking water, loungers, and hammocks are to confirm with the owners.

Off-the-grid basics

The island is proudly eco-friendly and off-grid, operating with a focus on sustainability. Power is generated on-site, and charging options for devices are to confirm.

Wi-Fi and phone signal are to confirm. Bathroom and shower facilities are to confirm — please check with the owners before your visit.

Best time to visit

Belize’s dry season runs from November to April and offers warm, sunny days — this is generally the best time to visit. The rainy season lasts from May to October, with typically sunny mornings and afternoon showers.

Champagne Island is a nature-focused destination, so expect lush greenery and active wildlife during the greener months.

Safety

The boat ride from Placencia is short and scenic, but life jackets and safety equipment are to confirm with the operators. Swimming conditions in the lagoon are to confirm.

Wildlife is part of the experience — do not approach or feed animals, and follow your guide’s instructions. First aid kits and emergency procedures are to confirm with the owners.

Good to know

Champagne Island welcomes day trippers, campers, families, and groups — the island can host up to 50 guests per group. The experience is not wheelchair accessible. Children must be accompanied by an adult.

Pet policies, accessibility details, and group size limits beyond 50 are to confirm. Dietary needs can likely be accommodated — please confirm with the owners when booking.

Booking and cancellation

To reserve your visit, contact Champagne Island directly via phone at +501 629-9979 or through their Tripadvisor listing. Deposits, payment methods, and the full cancellation policy are to confirm with the owners.

We recommend booking in advance, especially during the dry season (November to April).

Directions

Pickup point: Placencia Municipal Pier & Plaza, GJ7P+93G, Placencia Sidewalk, Placencia, Belize.

Landmark: The pier is located at the end of the Placencia Sidewalk — look for the main dock where boats depart for lagoon tours. If you reach the Placencia Plaza, you’re in the right spot.

Boat ride: Approximately 5–10 minutes across the Placencia lagoon. Your guide (often Ernest) will point out manatees, birds, and the mangrove ecosystem along the way.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get to Champagne Island?

Take a short boat ride from the Placencia Municipal Pier. The trip takes about 5–10 minutes through the lagoon.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is to confirm with the owners. Contact +501 629-9979 for current rates.

What should I bring?

Sun protection, bug spray, water shoes, cash, a reusable water bottle, and a dry bag.

Is food provided?

Authentic Garifuna food is available — Ms. Jackie is known for her cooking. Meal packages and pricing are to confirm.

Can I stay overnight?

Yes — camping and weekend accommodation for volunteers are offered. Details on bedding and amenities are to confirm.

Is there Wi-Fi or phone signal?

This is to confirm with the owners. As an off-grid island, connectivity may be limited.

Is Champagne Island family-friendly?

Yes — children are welcome but must be accompanied by an adult. The butterfly house and nature trails are popular with kids.

What activities are available?

Guided nature trails, medicinal and fruit trails, birding, fishing, water rentals, music and cultural food experiences, chocolate making, and private events.

What is the cancellation policy?

To confirm with the owners. Please ask when booking.

Is the island accessible for wheelchairs?

No — the experience is not wheelchair accessible. Please contact the owners to discuss specific accessibility needs.
