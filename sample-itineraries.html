Plan 1

A Day on the Island

Day trippers who want to see the best of the island between breakfast and sunset.

  1. 9:00Boat from the mainland
  2. 9:45Welcome and island orientation
  3. 10:00Guided nature trail
  4. 11:30Fruit trail, tasting as you go
  5. 12:30Lunch from the clay oven
  6. 14:00Water rentals: paddle or float along the shore
  7. 15:30Chocolate making workshop
  8. 17:00Live music and cultural food as the day cools
  9. 18:00Boat back to the mainland

Services used

Nature trailsFruit trailsWater rentalsChocolate makingMusic and cultural food

Good to know

Bring sunscreen, a hat, a swimsuit and comfortable walking shoes. Great for solo visitors, couples, friends or small families.

Plan 2

A Relaxing Weekend

Couples or friends (2 nights) who want slow mornings, good food and time to unwind.

Friday

  1. 16:00Boat from the mainland and settle in
  2. 18:30Dinner with music and cultural food

Saturday

  1. 6:30Birding at sunrise
  2. 8:30Slow breakfast
  3. 10:00Medicinal trail with a guide
  4. 12:30Lunch from the clay oven
  5. 14:30Fishing, or water rentals if you prefer to drift
  6. 17:00Chocolate making
  7. 19:00Dinner and live music

Sunday

  1. 9:00Late breakfast and a quiet walk
  2. 11:00Free time on the water
  3. 13:00Boat to the mainland

Services used

BirdingMedicinal trailsFishingWater rentalsChocolate makingMusic and cultural food

Good to know

Pack light layers, binoculars if you have them, and insect repellent. Perfect for 2 to 4 guests.

Plan 3

A Family or Group Retreat Weekend

Families, friend groups and teams (2 nights) who want shared adventures and room to gather.

Friday

  1. 15:00Group boat from the mainland
  2. 17:00Welcome gathering and settling in
  3. 19:00Group dinner with music and cultural food

Saturday

  1. 8:00Breakfast together
  2. 9:30Guided nature trail and fruit trail for all ages
  3. 12:30Clay oven lunch
  4. 14:00Choose your activity: fishing, birding or water rentals
  5. 16:30Group chocolate making
  6. 19:00Feast and live music

Sunday

  1. 9:00Relaxed breakfast
  2. 10:30Closing circle, or a final dip and paddle
  3. 13:00Boat to the mainland
