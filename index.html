About us

A little getaway with a lot of Belize in it.

Champagne Island is a perfect little getaway, a unique niche and a true paradise. We have all the basic amenities for your comfort, while still giving you an interactive taste of the simple life, with activities for every age and interest.

We are a small, locally owned and operated destination, and we are proud of our eco-friendly practices. Our aim is to share Belizean culture and natural beauty with every visitor, through guided tours, and as a private venue for special events and retreats.

Welcome to Champagne Island, the perfect escape to enhance your Belizean experience.

Experiences

Things to do

Slow down, or join in. Everything here is hands-on, family-friendly and rooted in Belize.

Culture, music and food

Hear the beat of the Garífuna drums and the rhythm of Maya marimba. Taste flavor-filled meals from our handmade clay oven and smoky fire hearth, and help in the cooking if you like.

  • Traditional dances and live music
  • Cook traditional Belizean meals with us
  • Make your own chocolate with traditional tools and fresh cacao
  • Visit our Garífuna house for a crash course and delicious cuisine

Trails, gardens and wildlife

Our handmade walkway brings a complete rainforest experience right to the island, with a wide diversity of plants, herbs and fruit trees to discover.

  • Nature, medicinal and fruit trails with licensed guides
  • Butterfly sanctuary with colorful species
  • Viewing deck for turtles, iguanas and birds
  • Listen for birds from the dock

On the water

Forget kayaks. Take a trip out on the lagoon in a dory, a boat carved from a tree and traditionally used to navigate Belize's rivers.

  • Recreational fishing the whole family can enjoy
  • Water recreation rentals
  • Fresh coconuts, cut by you

Stay the night

We are your ideal destination to camp away from the mainland, with the amenities and space for your adventure. Fall asleep to the lagoon and wake to a sunrise that is always the best.

  • Overnight accommodation and upscale camping
  • Waterfront hammocks and loungers
  • Open spaces for games and sports

Celebrations and retreats

Bring your family and celebrate milestones in a private space. Our thatch palapa, waterfront and open grounds make a setting people remember.

  • Private venue for birthdays and special events

  • Our friendly staff help with everything you need
